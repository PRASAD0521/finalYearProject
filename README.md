# Ethical Hacking Simulator With Risk Scoring Using Cybersecurity Techniques

## Paper Title
**Ethical Hacking Simulator With Risk Scoring Using Cybersecurity Techniques**

---

## Abstract

In the rapidly evolving digital landscape, cybersecurity threats have become increasingly sophisticated and frequent, creating a critical need for proactive and effective defense mechanisms.

This project presents the design and development of an **Ethical Hacking Simulator with Risk Scoring Using Cybersecurity Techniques**, aimed at enhancing security awareness, identifying system vulnerabilities, and promoting hands-on learning within a safe and controlled environment.

The simulator emulates real-world network infrastructures, enabling users to conduct ethical hacking activities such as **reconnaissance, vulnerability scanning, exploitation, and post-exploitation analysis**. These simulated attack scenarios provide practical exposure to penetration testing techniques without compromising real systems.

To complement vulnerability discovery, the system integrates an **automated risk assessment and scoring mechanism** that evaluates the potential impact of identified vulnerabilities. The **dynamic Risk Scoring Model** considers key factors such as exploitability, severity, asset value, and threat likelihood, offering meaningful insights into overall cyber risk.

The platform incorporates widely recognized cybersecurity frameworks and best practices, including the **OWASP Top 10**, **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**, structured penetration testing methodologies, and real-time attack simulation techniques. Risk scores are derived using **CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System)** metrics and are further enhanced through adaptive contextual scoring to reflect asset exposure and environmental risk factors.

This project serves as both an **educational tool** and a **cyber risk assessment platform**, supporting cybersecurity education, security auditing, and informed decision-making for defensive security strategies.
